# Bob Lazar & John Lear — Alien Species and Star-System Claims

_Compiled 2026-08-27. This records what each person CLAIMED, not what is true._

Hand-verified against primary sources. Records WHAT EACH PERSON CLAIMED, not what is true. claimant: lazar | lear | lear_circle (docs Lear co-wrote/circulated: Krill papers 1988, plus Valerian/Branton syntheses that get mis-attributed to him) . origin_attribution: explicit = the claimant names the origin for that being in that source; inferred = implied by context; derivative = comes from Lear-circle / later lore, not the claimant's own words; none = no origin given.

## TL;DR

- **Lazar** names one species: the ~3.5–4 ft Greys ("the Kids") from **Zeta2 Reticuli**, planet **"Reticulum 4"** (1989/1991) or **"ZR3"** (2019). His briefing 'books' are mostly lore about humans: 'containers', 'externally corrected evolution' with 65 corrections, Earth = 'Sol 3', non-Grey 'messengers' seeded among us, ~10,000-yr involvement, a 1979 altercation.
- **Lear's own list** (letter of Dec 13 1987): Grey-1/2/3, Blonds-Swedes-Nordics, Interdimensional, Hairy Dwarfs, Very Tall Race, human-appearing Drones, MIBs. He gives a home star for only the Greys: **Zeta Reticuli 1 & 2 (~38 ly)**. By 1990 he says Greys are 'cybernetic organisms… not a species… work for someone else.'
- Orion / Rigel / Bellatrix / Pleiades / Procyon / Sirius / Draco attributions come from the Krill papers (1988, Lear + John Grace), Valerian's *Matrix* (1988), Andrews (1993) and Branton — tagged **lear_circle / derivative**.

## Species and beings

### Reticulan Greys ('the Kids')

- **Claimant:** `lazar`  ·  **Category:** species  ·  **Confidence:** 0.9
- **Aliases:** the Kids, Greys, Grays, Zeta Reticulans, Reticulans, Zetas
- **Claimed origin:** Zeta Reticuli (binary: Zeta1 + Zeta2 Reticuli, ~39 ly, Reticulum) (attribution: explicit)
- **Planet:** 'Reticulum 4' — 4th planet from Zeta2 Reticuli (1989 regression, 1991 Lazar Tape). In 2019 (JRE #1315) he said 'ZR3', the 3rd planet.
- **Physical:** ~3.5–4 ft tall; large head; large eyes; craft interior sized for children (seats/consoles). Briefing docs allegedly contained autopsy photos; Lazar says he only saw pictures, never a live one for certain.
- **Behavior / role:** Telepathic. Docs said they can remotely anesthetize/immobilize a human by acting on the brain. Involved with Earth ~10,000 yrs; 'cooperative exchange' with the US until a 1979 altercation at S-4 in which guards/scientists were killed; docs gave a 'schedule of time for this group to come back'. Lazar thinks he glimpsed one at S-4 through a window (uncertain).
- **Relationship to humans:** Docs referred to Earth as 'Sol 3' and to humans as 'containers'. Humans are the product of 'externally corrected evolution' — 65 corrections performed by the Greys. Greys are 'afraid of' human higher thinking; most of the brain 'was supposed to be contained'. Greys seeded non-Grey 'messengers' among humans (see lazar_planted_messengers).
- **Caveats:** Lazar consistently says the origin/biology material was 'just in the paperwork', possibly seeded disinformation to trace leaks, and that he only vouches for the reactor/propulsion. The 'religion book' details come from a hypnosis session shared by John Lear; Lazar later distanced himself from that material.
- > "These beings are from Reticulum 4 which is the 4th planet out from Zeta 2 Reticuli." — [The Lazar Tape… and Excerpts from the Government Bible (video, 1991)](https://www.meta-religion.com/Paranormale/UFO/Bob_lazar/zeta_2_reticuli.htm)
- > "It was what they called ZR3. So it was a third planet in that star system." — [Joe Rogan Experience #1315 — Bob Lazar & Jeremy Corbell](https://singjupost.com/transcript-bob-lazar-jeremy-corbell-on-joe-rogan-podcast-1315/)
- > "Zeta Reticuli. The star system there. Its the second star. Reticulum 4." — [Bob Lazar hypnotic-regression transcript (Layne Keck), Aug 10 1989](https://www.papooselake.org/documents/1989-08-10-bob-lazar-hypnotic-regression-transcript)

### Grey-1 (EBE)

- **Claimant:** `lear`  ·  **Category:** species  ·  **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Aliases:** EBE, EBEs, Extraterrestrial Biological Entities, Greys, the little greys, EBE-1/EBE-2/EBE-3 (captives)

Atrophied, non-functional digestive system; feed by absorbing an enzyme/hormonal secretion (from cattle/human tissue) mixed with hydrogen peroxide through the skin; excrete waste through the skin. Communicate telepathically.
- **Behavior / role:** 'Worship technology. Don't give a damn about mankind.' Perform abductions (3 mm brain implant via nasal cavity; post-hypnotic suggestion for an event 2–5 yrs out; crossbreeding) and cattle/human mutilations. Claim to have created Christ; possess a holographic recorder of Earth history. 1990 update: 'all types of Greys… definitely cybernetic organisms… not a species themselves. They work for someone else… operate from higher orders.'
- **Relationship to humans:** Hostile/indifferent; 'Grand Deception' of MJ-12; 'the invasion is essentially complete'. Three living captives designated EBE-1, EBE-2, EBE-3 at YY-II, Los Alamos.
- **Caveats:** EBE name attributed by Lear to Detlev Bronk (MJ-12). Much of this is Lear relaying Paul Bennewitz / 'Dulce Papers' / Bill Moore material.
