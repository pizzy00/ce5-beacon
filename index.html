CE-5 PROTOCOL — FIELD CARD ✕
WHAT IT IS
A Close Encounter of the Fifth Kind is contact that people initiate on purpose instead of waiting for it. The term and the practice come from Dr. Steven Greer and CSETI (early 1990s). What follows is a plain-language summary in our own words.
THE CORE STEPS
01SETTLESit outside, ideally with a group, at a dark-sky spot away from city light. Get comfortable, screens dim, fear off.
02GO QUIETA few minutes of slow breathing and stillness. Let thought settle until awareness feels open and wide. (The BREATHE IN / OUT cue here paces this.)
03VECTORPicture the galaxy, find the Sun, find Earth, then descend in your mind to the exact spot you are sitting. Hold a clear, welcoming invitation: you are welcome here. (This console's flight path is a guided version of this step.)
04SIGNALA bright light in slow, deliberate flashes, or tones played into the night. Signals say where you are and that you mean it. (TONE runs a soft 528 / 432 Hz carrier.)
05STAY OPENKeep watching without straining. Note lights, flashes, orbs, flashbulb events, or a felt sense of presence. Do not chase. Stay calm.
06LOG ITTime, direction, duration, weather, and what everyone saw or felt. Repeat over weeks; consistency beats one big night.
07CLOSE WITH THANKSEnd with gratitude and a clear goodbye.
GROUND RULES
Consent and peaceful intent are the whole point. No fear, no demands.
One shared intention on the same timing beats a big headcount.
Typical session: 1 to 2 hours on a clear night, darker is better.
Stay safe and legal: a spot you are allowed to be at night, and never point a laser at an aircraft.
HOW THIS CONSOLE MAPS TO IT
Departure system = who you are inviting · Their star → Galaxy → Sol → Luna → Earth → descent = the vectoring visualization · Breath cue = step 02 · TONE = step 04 · The beacon = we are here. LUNA and DEEP OCEAN run the same steps from a closer origin (UAP / USO). BERMUDA runs the trigger sequence: the Triangle, the places we detonated, the flash (close your eyes; the screen becomes the light of a real shot, measured from the film), the arsenal, then the unit rises.
